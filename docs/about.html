What is FIRST & FLL?

A quick explainer for families who are brand new to FIRST robotics.

🚀 What is FIRST?

FIRST® (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a national nonprofit that runs hands-on STEM programs for kids from preschool through high school. FIRST LEGO League (FLL) is their program for younger kids, built around LEGO bricks and robotics.

🧩 What is FLL Explore?

FLL has a few age divisions. Explore is the one for our age group (K–2, roughly ages 5–7) — it's the hands-on, low-pressure introduction to engineering and coding, focused on discovery and teamwork rather than competitive scoring. Kids build a LEGO model and share what they learned with judges at an event, and everyone is celebrated for their work.

Older kids (grades 4+) move on to FLL Challenge, which is the competitive robot-game version most people picture when they think of "robotics competitions."

🌱 What is BIOGLOW?

Every FLL season has a science theme, and this year's is BIOGLOW™ — it's all about ecosystems: how plants, animals, fungi, and people all depend on each other to keep the world healthy. Our team will explore that theme through building, a bit of coding, and a simple research project.

Because it's brand new, official season materials for our age group aren't released yet (LEGO Education/FIRST has said October 2026), so some format details — like exactly what an event day looks like — are still TBD even for us.
