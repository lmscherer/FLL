Meet your coach

Coach Lindsay Scherer

Hi! I'm Lindsay, and I'm so excited to be coaching the Anaheim Astromechs this season — my own two kids will be on the team. This is my first season coaching in California, and my first time back in FLL since before COVID, so I'm just as excited as the kids to jump back in.

💼 Professional background

I hold a BSE in Industrial Engineering (Arizona State University, 2016) and an MBA (Arizona State University, 2019). Since 2015 I've worked in software quality management — 7 years at SAP (2015–2022) and, since 2022, at Apple. Most days that means a lot of process and problem-solving — we'll see how well that translates to a room of 4–6-year-old engineers!

🤖 FIRST & STEM volunteer history

2009–2020
Annual FLL volunteer across Arizona — scrimmages, regional events, and state competitions, in roles including EmCee, robot inspector, referee, and volunteer coordinator.
2011
FRC (FIRST Robotics Competition) team mentor — helped my team take home the Spirit Award that season.
2011–2012
Won Volunteer of the Year at the Arizona Chandler Regional FLL event.
2013–2016
VEX Robotics volunteer for AZ VEX IQ and VEX Robotics Competition (VRC) events — set-up/take-down, robot inspector, and EmCee. Won AZ Volunteer of the Year for VEX during this span.
2014–2015
VEX IQ EmCee at VEX Robotics World Championship, two years running.
2014–2016
FLL team mentor (1 season), then team coach (2 seasons) for an Arizona charter school team.
2024–2025
Helped Fairmont Historic Anaheim Campus launch the FLL Explore Classroom Pack in their pre-K curriculum.

✨ Why I'm coaching

I've spent most of my FIRST years on the event side — judging, refereeing, EmCee-ing — and I loved watching teams grow over a season. Now I get to see that process from the coach and parent seat, with my own kids on the team. I'm looking forward to building something fun (and a little chaotic, in the best way) with this group.
